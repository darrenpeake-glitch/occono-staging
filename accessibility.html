Accessibility statement

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Our aim

Occono aims to make this website usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using keyboards, screen readers, magnification, or reduced-motion settings.

What we have done

  • Used semantic headings and short pages of text.
  • Written link text that describes the destination.
  • Kept layouts readable when text is zoomed or the screen is narrow.
  • Used colour combinations intended to provide readable contrast.

Known limitations

Articles link out to third-party tool websites. Those sites are outside Occono’s control and may be harder to use than these pages.

Feedback

Report an accessibility problem to hello@occono.co.uk. Please name the page and the difficulty.

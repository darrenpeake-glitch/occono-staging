Accessibility statement

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Our aim

Occono aims to make this website usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using keyboards, screen readers, magnification or reduced-motion settings.

What we have done

  • Used semantic headings and landmarks.
  • Provided visible keyboard focus styles.
  • Used labelled form controls and status messaging.
  • Designed responsive layouts for zoom and smaller screens.
  • Respected reduced-motion preferences where animation is used.
  • Used colour combinations intended to provide readable contrast.

Known limitations

The portfolio includes concept-site previews and third-party photography. Further assistive-technology testing and final alternative-text review will be completed before commercial launch.

Feedback

Report an accessibility problem to hello@occono.co.uk once the business inbox is active. Please describe the page and the difficulty encountered.

How this comparison was made

The short version

A UK online fitness coach who sells from a laptop usually has four jobs that get mixed into one software search. The first is a page that can take money for a programme or a course. The second is a letter that keeps past clients in reach. The third is the admin after someone fills in a check-in: a row on a sheet, and an email that goes out without being retyped. The fourth is the diary, a link a client uses to book one session. Systeme.io’s free plan covers the page, the course and a weekly broadcast. The comparison script sets Email newsletters to Unlimited on that plan. The FAQ’s 1 email campaign is a different row, for an automated sequence. beehiiv’s free plan is the letter when you want that as its own product. Make’s free plan covers the check-in.

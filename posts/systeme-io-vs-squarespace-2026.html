The short version

Squarespace is a website builder with hosting, templates and a store. Systeme.io is an all-in-one for funnels, email, a course and checkout. The first bill is the difference. Systeme.io’s free plan is $0, and its top published plan in this check is $97 a month. Squarespace has no free plan. The trial is 14 days, with no credit card required. After that, a GB website plan in pricing.txt starts at £16 a month on Basic, or £144 for the year.

The second difference is email. Systeme.io’s visible FAQ says unlimited email sends on every plan, including free, with the free plan capped at 1 email campaign. Squarespace Email Campaigns is billed separately from the website plan, by monthly sends rather than by list size. This check did not record a verified price for that product, so none is printed. The third difference is the cut on a digital sale. Systeme.io’s pricing page says 0% transaction fees and does not print Stripe’s or PayPal’s rates. Squarespace’s feature comparison charges a Digital Content and Memberships fee of 7%, 5%, 1% or 0% by plan, and it shows card rates in dollars beside that.

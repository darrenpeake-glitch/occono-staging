The short version

Systeme.io is an all-in-one for funnels, email, a course and checkout, priced as a monthly plan in US dollars, with a free plan underneath. Gumroad is a shop for digital products, memberships and courses, with no monthly charge. Gumroad’s bill is a percentage of the sale. The first difference is that bill. Systeme.io’s free plan is $0, and its top published plan in the visible FAQ is $97 a month. Gumroad’s pricing page says “No hidden fees, no monthly charges.” The fees help then lists credit card processing and PayPal fees as not included in the 10% + $0.50. Both sentences are on Gumroad’s site. This article prints both.

The second difference is the sale itself. Systeme.io’s pricing page says 0% transaction fees, and Stripe or PayPal at no extra cost from Systeme.io. It does not print those processors’ rates. Gumroad’s pricing page prints 10% + $0.50 for a direct sale and 30% for Discover. The fees help adds 2.9% + $0.30 for cards on the direct rate, and says the 30% already includes processing. The third difference is tax on a UK digital sale. Gumroad’s VAT help says Gumroad is the merchant of record and collects and remits VAT on digital sales to UK buyers. Systeme.io’s UK VAT help says a UK-based seller is charged 20% on digital sales to UK customers. Those are different jobs. Neither figure is turned into a pound total below.

The short version

A UK independent consultant usually has three jobs that get mixed into one software search. The first is a letter that keeps past clients in reach. The second is a page that can take money for a workshop, a diagnostic or a short PDF. The third is the admin after someone enquires: a row on a sheet, and an email that goes out without being retyped. beehiiv, Systeme.io and Make each publish a $0 plan that covers one of those jobs. None of the three, on the pages read for this article, covers all three jobs on the free plan in the same login.

The prices are in US dollars, as the vendors publish them. No page in this check printed a pound price or a UK VAT rate to add. A UK card issuer may still add a conversion charge. This article does not convert the figures.

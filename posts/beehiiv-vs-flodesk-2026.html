Quick summary

Send on beehiiv while the list is still inside the free cap. Pay Flodesk when the letter needs a sequence, the logo off, or a list that will not stay under 2,500.

Best when the letter is a broadcast and the list is still under 2,500 people: beehiiv. The free plan is $0, with no card, up to 2,500 subscribers and unlimited sends. The visible free card lists the send. Flodesk’s FAQ, read the same day, says its free plan does not. We earn from beehiiv.

Best when you will pay, and you want one price that does not move when the list grows: Flodesk. From 10 November 2026 the paid plans a new member can buy are Unlimited Email and Unlimited Everything. The pricing page says a 14-day trial, with no card. We do not earn from Flodesk.

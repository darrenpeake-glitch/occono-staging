Quick summary

Stay on the larger free list, and pay for a plan only when the percentage costs more

Best for broadcasts, and for selling a file before a monthly plan: Kit, formerly ConvertKit. The free plan is $0 for as long as the list stays under 10,000 subscribers. It includes unlimited broadcasts, forms and landing pages. Commerce, including a paid newsletter, is on every plan, including Free. A sale in pounds costs 3.5% + £0.20. We do not earn from Kit.

Best when the letter is also a site, and paid subscriptions are large enough that a percentage would cost more than the plan: beehiiv. The free plan is $0, with no card, up to 2,500 subscribers and unlimited sends. The pricing FAQ says the website, podcast, community and digital products are on every plan, and that beehiiv takes 0% of paid subscription and digital product revenue. Paid subscriptions are listed on the Lite card, not on Free. With the page set to 1,000 subscribers and yearly billing, on 22 October 2026, Lite showed $49 a month. We earn from beehiiv.

Kit’s subscription is the smaller one if you already need a paid plan at that list size. beehiiv’s 0% cut is the smaller bill only after Kit’s percentage on each sale is larger than Lite, and only while the whole list is still in a tier whose price this page could read.

A worked example, Occono’s own arithmetic. It uses the dollar prices above. It ignores tax and any currency-conversion charge a UK card issuer adds. One hundred payments of $10 is $1,000. Kit’s stated fee is 3.5% + $0.30 on each payment: 3.5% of $1,000 is $35, and 100 times $0.30 is $30, so the fee is $65. The plan is $0 if the whole list is under 10,000. beehiiv’s stated platform cut of that $1,000 is $0, and Lite at the up-to-1,000 yearly card is $49. beehiiv’s page also quotes Stripe at 2.9% + $0.30. On these 100 payments that quote is $29 plus $30, which is $59. The plan plus that Stripe quote is $108. Kit’s $65 is the smaller bill in this example, if the 3.5% + $0.30 is the whole cost of the sale, which is what Kit’s commerce article says.

The same arithmetic meets when the gap covers the plan. Kit’s fee on a $10 payment is $0.65. The Stripe figure beehiiv quotes on a $10 payment is $0.59. The difference is $0.06. $49 divided by $0.06 is about 817. At 816 payments of $10, which is $8,160, the two bills are within a few cents. Under that, Kit’s percentage is the smaller bill. Over that, Lite plus the quoted Stripe figure is smaller. This only holds while every subscriber, paying or not, is still inside the up-to-1,000 yearly price of $49. 816 paying subscribers already use most of that bucket. This article does not have a verified Lite price above 1,000 subscribers.

A pound example, still this article’s arithmetic, for someone who is not converting currencies. Forty payments of £10 is £400. Kit’s pound fee is 3.5% + £0.20: 3.5% of £400 is £14, and 40 times £0.20 is £8, so the fee is £22. That sale can sit on Kit’s free plan. beehiiv Lite is priced in dollars. This article does not turn $49 into pounds.

Which one fits you?

Wren W.’s June 2026 review is a one-person course business that wanted the sequence to run after the form. Dru R.’s review, the same month, is the bill that rose with the list and stayed up. Alexander’s December 2024 review is a UK media business that left beehiiv because the emails were not arriving, and that went back to Kit. Crystal’s June 2026 review is a writer who wanted the letter and the product on one site, and who still found the editor slow. Those four are closer to a one-person publisher than a software-buyer average is.

A tutor, bookkeeper or shop sending one letter a month to people who already buy. You are not charging for the letter. Both free plans cover the send. Kit’s cap is 10,000 subscribers. beehiiv’s is 2,500. A local list will not hit 2,500 quickly, and it will not hit 10,000 quickly either. Kit is the one with more room if the list is already a few thousand. Neither takes a cut if nobody is paying for the newsletter. If the letter should also add the enquiry to a sheet you already keep, Make can connect the form to the sheet. That is a second product, and a second bill once you leave Make’s free plan. Kit’s free plan will host the form and a broadcast. The sequence that chases the enquiry is not on that free plan.

A creator selling a short download, or a paid letter, to a list that is still small. Kit’s commerce article puts the product on every plan, including Free, and states the pound fee as 3.5% + £0.20. Forty sales at £10 cost £22 on that table, and the software plan is still $0 while the list is under 10,000. beehiiv’s FAQ says digital products are on every plan and that the platform cut is 0%, then the offer data lists digital products on Lite’s additions. Paid subscriptions are on the Lite card. Until those two beehiiv lines agree, Kit is the page that clearly lets you take the payment before a monthly plan. Crystal’s review is the case for beehiiv once you want the file and the letter on the site the FAQ describes. You still pay the Stripe figure beehiiv quotes, which is a US-style 2.9% + $0.30, not a UK rate.

A coach who needs one follow-up sequence, not only a broadcast. Kit’s free plan does not include the sequence. Creator starts at $39 a month even if you have a few hundred subscribers, because the published entry price is the up-to-1,000 tier. Wren W.’s review is that plan doing the job: new sign-ups drop into a sequence. beehiiv lists automations on Lite, which showed $49 a month on yearly billing at 1,000 subscribers, or $59 in the monthly offer data. $39 against $59 is Kit, if the only gap is the sequence and you are paying month to month. $390 against $588, on the yearly arithmetic above, is still Kit. Pay beehiiv for the sequence if you also want the website, the podcast and the 0% cut the FAQ describes, and if the sales arithmetic further up has already crossed. If the sequence is attached to a sales page and a course, a newsletter tool is the wrong stack. The Kit and Systeme.io comparison is that page, and Systeme.io is the all-in-one this site uses for a page, email and a course.

On the dollar example above, that fee stays under beehiiv Lite plus beehiiv’s own Stripe quote until roughly 816 payments of $10 in a month, and those 816 people already crowd the 1,000-subscriber price this article could read. Creator at $39 a month is also the smaller subscription if you need sequences at that tier.

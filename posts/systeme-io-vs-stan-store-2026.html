The short version

Systeme.io is an all-in-one for funnels, email, a course and checkout, priced as a monthly plan in US dollars, with a free plan that caps contacts, courses, one automated email sequence and lifetime sales. A newsletter is a different row, and the comparison script sets that row to Unlimited on every plan, including Free. Stan Store is a link-in-bio store for digital products, courses, bookings, memberships and a community, priced as a flat subscription in US dollars, with no free plan and no platform percentage. The first difference is the bill before anyone buys. Systeme.io’s free plan is $0 and needs no card. Stan’s pricing post says the way in is a 14-day trial, then Creator at $29 a month or Creator Pro at $99 a month. A quiet month does not shrink Stan’s subscription. It also does not create a Systeme.io bill while the free caps still hold.

The second difference is what the $29 buys, and what it does not. Stan’s help article puts an unlimited course builder, calendar bookings, recurring subscriptions, lead magnets, a community and Instagram AutoDM on Creator. It puts discount codes, order bumps, funnels, affiliate management, email broadcasts, email flows and pixel tracking on Creator Pro. Systeme.io’s FAQ puts 3 funnels, 1 course and 1 email campaign on the free plan, 5 courses on Startup at $17, 20 courses plus evergreen webinars on Webinar at $47, and unlimited courses on Unlimited at $97. That email campaign is one automated sequence. Newsletters are Unlimited on every plan, including Free. Stan puts email broadcasts on Creator Pro at $99. Systeme.io’s free plan already sends them.

The third difference is the card fee a UK seller can actually read. Stan’s fees article prints a UK Stripe standard purchase fee of 1.5% + £0.20, and a Stan platform fee of 0%. Systeme.io’s pricing page says 0% transaction fees and Stripe or PayPal at no extra cost from Systeme.io. It does not print Stripe’s UK rate, so this article states none for a Systeme.io sale. Both subscription tables are in dollars. Neither showed a euro price. This article does not convert $29 into pounds.

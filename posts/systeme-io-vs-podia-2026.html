Quick summary

Use the free plan until someone pays. Pay for Podia when the site itself is what you are selling.

Best when you are still finding out if a course or a download will sell: Systeme.io. The free plan is $0, needs no card, and has no end date. It includes one course with up to 500 students, up to 2,000 contacts, and up to $10,000 in total sales. The pricing data says 0% transaction fees, and that you can take Stripe or PayPal at no extra platform cost. Startup is $17 a month. We earn from Systeme.io. The solo-plan write-up is Is Systeme.io worth it for solopreneurs?

Best when you want courses, downloads and a community on one site, and you will pay from the first month: Podia. There is no free plan on the pricing page. There is a 14-day trial, and the page says you do not enter a card or pick a plan until you are ready. Mover is $49 a month, or $42 a month billed annually ($504 a year), with a 5% fee on each sale. Shaker removes that fee, at $99 a month or $84 a month billed annually ($1,008 a year). We do not earn from Podia.

Podia is the dearer subscription at every published step a solo creator is likely to start on. It is the better buy when a tidy site, a download product and a community matter more than a zero monthly bill. If the thing you actually send is a newsletter, neither tool is the one to start with.

A worked example, Occono’s own arithmetic. It uses the dollar prices above. It ignores card conversion, VAT on the subscription, and the processor’s fee. Twelve times Systeme.io Startup at $17 is $204. That is the monthly price held for a year. It is not an annual-billing total. Twelve times Podia Mover at $49 is $588. The annual total on the page is $504, which is $84 less, and it still carries the 5% fee. Twelve times Shaker at $99 is $1,188. The annual total is $1,008. The gap between Mover annual and Shaker annual is $1,008 minus $504, which is $504. A 5% fee reaches $504 when sales reach $10,080. Below that, a year of Mover plus the 5% costs less than a year of Shaker, before you count PayPal, upsells and the higher product cap. Above that, Shaker annual costs less than Mover annual plus the fee. Systeme.io’s stated platform fee on Startup is 0%, so the $204 does not grow with sales. The processor still takes a cut. The $204 is not the only money that leaves the sale.

One course at $97, sold to 10 people, is $970. That sits inside the free plan’s $10,000 sales cap, and inside the 500-student cap. It does not cover a year of Podia Mover at $504, before the 5%. Five percent of $970 is $48.50, so a year of Mover annual plus that fee is $552.50, against $0 of Systeme.io platform fee on the free plan, or $204 if you are already on Startup. At $20 a download, 500 sales is $10,000, so the free plan’s sales cap and a 500-student course cap can arrive together. A creator store can list more than one download. The free plan’s course cap is still one course. The $10,000 cap is total sales, not sales of one product.

Where Podia is the better buy

Pick Podia when you would rather pay than assemble the shop. Courses, downloads, a community, a website, a blog, landing pages and a custom domain are on Mover, not held for a higher plan. Leslie E.’s review is a coach who describes the screens as easy. Lucas B.’s September 2026 review, also on Capterra, is a founder who hosts courses clients watch on their own, plus spaces for one-to-one and one-to-many work, and who gives ease of use 5.0. Brenda F’s review is a website stood up in a weekend, with a sales page created when a product is added. Capterra’s 4.6 from 126 reviews is the public score that matches that praise. You pay for it from the first month after the trial. There is no free plan on the pricing page. Mover at $42 a month on annual billing is $504 a year, and the 5% is still there. Shaker, at $84 a month on annual billing, is the first plan that drops the Podia fee and adds PayPal.

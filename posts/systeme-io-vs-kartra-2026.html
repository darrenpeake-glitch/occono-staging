The short version

Systeme.io and Kartra both try to replace a stack of separate tools: landing pages, sales funnels, checkout, email, courses and memberships. The difference that matters to most UK sole traders is the bill. Systeme.io starts free and its top plan is $97 a month. Kartra starts at $59 a month and its top plan is $549 a month billed monthly.

Kartra does include things Systeme.io’s pricing page does not list, such as SMS sending, a customer helpdesk on Growth and above, and help-desk live chat on Professional. If you will not use those, you are paying for them anyway.

The offer

Systeme.io: free plan, $0, no card, up to 2,000 contacts. Startup is $17 a month. Annual billing is advertised as 2 months free. Open a free Systeme.io account.

Kartra: no free plan. 14-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee, as advertised on the pricing page. Essentials is $59 a month, or $52 a month billed annually. Kartra’s pricing page.

What the gap looks like over a year, using only the monthly prices above: a list of 2,500 contacts fits Systeme.io Startup at $17 a month, or Kartra Starter at $119 a month. That is $204 a year against $1,428, a difference of $1,224. On Kartra’s annual billing, Starter is $99 a month, or $1,188 a year. Your card issuer may add a foreign-exchange charge on a dollar subscription.

On Kartra Essentials the 5% transaction fee also counts. If you sold £1,000 of coaching or courses in a month, that would be £50 to Kartra, on top of whatever your payment processor charges.

Which one fits you?

A Plymouth life coach with 400 contacts and one £97 programme. Systeme.io’s free plan covers the list, the course and the checkout at $0, with no transaction fee. Kartra’s cheapest plan would be $59 a month plus 5% of each sale. Systeme.io, clearly.

A bookkeeper in Leeds selling a self-assessment mini course to 3,000 contacts. Systeme.io Startup at $17 a month covers 5,000 contacts and 5 courses. Kartra Starter covers 2,500 contacts, so this list would push you to Growth under Kartra’s FAQ rule. Systeme.io.

A fitness studio with 8,000 contacts that wants text reminders and a customer helpdesk. This is where Kartra makes a case: Growth lists SMS, a helpdesk and 12,500 contacts in one bill, at $229 a month or $189 billed annually. Systeme.io Webinar is $47 a month for 10,000 contacts, but its pricing page does not list SMS or a helpdesk, so you would add separate tools. Price both setups before you choose.

A consultant who wants other people to sell their course for commission. Systeme.io includes an affiliate programme on every plan, including free. Kartra lists affiliates from Growth. Systeme.io.

A writer whose business is really a weekly newsletter. Neither is the obvious pick. Have a look at beehiiv and our beehiiv vs Kit comparison first.

Where Kartra is the better buy

If you already earn enough that $189 to $429 a month is a small line in your accounts, and you want SMS, a helpdesk, live chat and real-time funnel analytics without stitching tools together, Kartra puts them in one login. Its reviewers praise exactly that. Its reviewers also say it takes time to learn and gets expensive as your list grows.

Where Systeme.io is the cheaper buy

For almost everyone starting out, and for most UK sole traders under 10,000 contacts. You can build the funnel, the course and the email sequence on the free plan, and only pay when your list passes 2,000 people. If you later need to pass sales into a spreadsheet, your accounts software or a CRM that Systeme.io does not connect to, an automation tool such as Make can sit alongside it.

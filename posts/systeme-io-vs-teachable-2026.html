The short version

Teachable is a course school: products, a student app, checkout, and tax tools. Systeme.io is an all-in-one: the course sits next to funnels, email, a simple affiliate programme and checkout. The bill is the first difference. Systeme.io’s free plan is $0 and its top published plan is $97 a month. Teachable’s cheapest card is $39 a month, and the 0% transaction-fee card is $89 a month.

The second difference is the cut on a sale. Systeme.io’s pricing page says 0% transaction fees on every plan, and that you accept Stripe or PayPal at no extra cost from Systeme.io. Teachable’s Starter card charges 7.5%. Builder, Growth and Custom drop that to 0% when you use Teachable Payments. Payment processors still charge their own fees on both sides. Teachable prints those processing rates. Systeme.io’s pricing page does not.

The offer

Systeme.io: free plan, $0, no card, up to 2,000 contacts and 1 course, with the 500-student limit and the $10,000 lifetime sales cap above. Startup is $17 a month. The yearly control is badged “2 months free”. Open a free Systeme.io account.

Teachable: no free plan. The cards and the FAQ say a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Starter is $39 a month, or $29 a month billed annually, with a 7.5% transaction fee. Builder is $89 a month, or $69 billed annually, with a 0% transaction fee when you use Teachable Payments. Teachable’s pricing page.

What the gap looks like over a year, using only the card prices, and labelled as our arithmetic. Systeme.io Startup is $17 × 12 = $204. Teachable Starter on monthly billing is $39 × 12 = $468. Teachable Starter on the annual card is $29 × 12 = $348. Builder monthly is $89 × 12 = $1,068. Builder annual is $69 × 12 = $828. The “Save $120”, “Save $240” and “Save $600” lines match those card gaps: $10, $20 and $50 a month, times 12. The FAQ’s “saves you 22%” matches Builder’s card more closely than Starter’s or Growth’s. On the cards, $29 is 25.6% below $39, $69 is 22.5% below $89, and $139 is 26.5% below $189. Your card issuer may still add a conversion charge.

The 7.5% on Teachable Starter is separate from the subscription. If you sold £1,000 of courses in a month, 7.5% is £75 to Teachable, before the processing rate on the pricing page and before the $39. Systeme.io’s own fee on that £1,000 would be $0 on the pricing page’s 0% rule. Stripe or PayPal can still charge you. This article does not have Systeme.io’s processor rates.

Teachable’s legacy overage rates, for schools still on that schedule: active students $0.10 a month each on Starter and Builder, and $0.05 on Growth and Advanced. Imported students $1.50, $1.00, $0.75 and $0.50 a month on those four names. Published products above 100 are $199 a month for every extra 50, on the legacy schedule. The current-policy section we read does not repeat that product fee. The legacy table says “Advanced”. The pricing cards say “Custom”. The page does not say those are the same plan, so this article does not treat them as the same plan.

Teachable’s usage-fees page says that, at the time of drafting, more than 80% of customers do not incur any usage fees. That is Teachable’s sentence, dated in the document as last updated 28 September 2026. It is not a figure Occono measured.

Which one fits you?

A Bristol yoga teacher with one £47 course and about 200 people on a list. Systeme.io’s free plan covers the course, the list and the checkout at $0, with a 0% platform fee, until the 500-student cap or the $10,000 lifetime sales cap gets in the way. Teachable Starter would be $39 a month plus 7.5% of each sale. Systeme.io.

A Cardiff bookkeeper selling a self-assessment course at £197, expecting about 15 sales a month. Fifteen sales is £2,955. Teachable’s 7.5% on that is £221.63, on top of $39, using only the Starter card. Moving to Builder to drop the 7.5% costs $89 a month, or $69 billed annually. Systeme.io Startup at $17 a month covers 5 courses and 5,000 contacts with a 0% platform fee, if the list is past 2,000 people. Under 2,000 contacts, the free plan still applies until lifetime sales pass $10,000. Systeme.io, unless the student app is the reason you are buying.

A Glasgow language tutor who wants students to open lessons in an iPhone app, and who sells to people in several countries. Teachable’s three priced cards all list iOS and Android student apps, and the tax tooltip names US sales tax, EU VAT and Canadian GST. It does not name UK VAT. Systeme.io’s pricing FAQ does not list a student app. If the app and the tax handling are the reason for the bill, Teachable Starter is the published starting point, and you still pay the 7.5% until Builder. Price that 7.5% against a year of sales before you stay on Starter.

A Norwich consultant who wants other people to sell the course for a commission. Systeme.io includes an affiliate programme on the free plan. Teachable lists affiliates from Builder, at $89 a month or $69 billed annually. Systeme.io, unless you already need Teachable for the reasons above.

A writer whose real product is a weekly newsletter, with a short course attached. Have a look at beehiiv and our beehiiv vs Substack comparison before you pay for a course school.

Someone already on Teachable who wants each sale copied into a spreadsheet or into their accounts. That job is an automation, not a reason to change course platform by itself. Make can sit beside either platform. Our Make vs Zapier guide is the page for that bill.

Where Teachable is the better buy

When the course is the business, and you want the student apps and the payments-and-tax line that Teachable prints on every priced card. Reviewers who like it talk about the course builder and checkout. Reviewers who leave talk about the price, support, and tools that feel thin once the school is a real business. Growth, at $189 a month or $139 billed annually, is the card that adds certificates and removal of Teachable branding. If those extras are the point, and the subscription is a small line in the accounts, Teachable is the fit. Confirm the trial length and the dollar amount on the order form, because the page disagrees with itself.

Where Systeme.io is the cheaper buy

For a UK sole trader who is still proving the course, and for most people under 2,000 contacts. You can put the course, the checkout and the emails on the free plan, and move to Startup at $17 a month when the list, the student cap or the $10,000 sales cap says you have to. You also get the funnel and the email tool in the same login, which is the gap Gabby W. flags on Teachable.

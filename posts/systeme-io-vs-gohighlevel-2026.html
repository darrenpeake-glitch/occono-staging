What the prices actually mean

Systeme.io is predictable. Free until 2,000 contacts. Startup at $17 covers most early growth. Even the top plan matches GoHighLevel’s entry price but without the usage add-ons. Annual billing is roughly two months free.

GoHighLevel starts higher and the real bill is usually higher still. The $97 Starter includes the core tools (CRM, funnels, email, SMS capability, booking), but SMS segments, call minutes and some AI features come out of a prepaid wallet.

Which one fits you?

You’re selling a course, digital product or simple coaching offer. You need a funnel, email sequence and checkout. Systeme.io’s free plan lets you build and test this with zero monthly cost. Most people in this position never need GoHighLevel’s extra features and would feel the $97+ as pure overhead.

You run an appointment-based local service (trades, clinics, consultants) and live on lead response speed. Two-way SMS and native booking matter. GoHighLevel is built for that workflow. The higher cost can pay for itself if missed calls and slow follow-up are currently costing you jobs.

You’re starting an agency and want to resell software or manage many client accounts. GoHighLevel’s sub-account architecture and white-label options on the higher plans are the point of the product. Systeme.io can handle basic sub-accounts but is not designed as an agency operating system.

Practical recommendation

If you are a solopreneur or small operator focused on funnels, email and digital products, start on the free plan of Systeme.io. Build one real offer. Only consider GoHighLevel if you hit a concrete gap (two-way SMS, calling, or multi-client management) that is already costing revenue.

Most one-person businesses never need to make that jump.

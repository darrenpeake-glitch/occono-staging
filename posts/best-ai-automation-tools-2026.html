Most tool lists are written for teams with budgets and specialists. This one is filtered for people running things largely alone.

How to choose

Start with the single highest-friction weekly task. Match it to one tool. Get that working and measuring time saved before adding the next.

Most people get better results from one solid system running reliably than from five half-configured tools.

Notes on the top options

n8n

Strongest option when you need multi-step logic that can research, decide, and act. Self-hosting keeps ongoing cost low once set up.

Make

Faster to start for many people. Excellent visual builder and good AI modules.

Claude Projects

Best current option for turning messy knowledge into clean systems, SOPs, and long-form thinking that stays consistent.

Cursor

Turns “I wish I had a small internal tool” into something you can actually ship without waiting on a developer.

Systeme.io

One of the strongest all-in-one options for solopreneurs who want funnels, email, and basic courses in a single platform. The free plan is genuinely usable and the economics are excellent if you later want to grow. Try Systeme.io →

The short version

Systeme.io is an all-in-one for funnels, email, a course and checkout, priced as a monthly plan in US dollars, with a free plan that caps contacts, campaigns and lifetime sales. Mailchimp is an email platform priced by how many contacts you store. The first difference is the free cap. Systeme.io’s visible FAQ gives the free plan 2,000 contacts and unlimited sends. Mailchimp’s rendered card gives the free plan 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with 250 sends a day. A list of past customers often crosses 250 before the business feels large. It can sit inside Systeme.io’s free plan for a long time after that.

The second difference is the shape of the paid bill. Systeme.io’s visible FAQ has three paid prices, and they do not move with the size of the list inside each plan: Startup $17 a month up to 5,000 contacts, Webinar $47 a month up to 10,000, Unlimited $97 a month with unlimited contacts. Mailchimp’s price file has a separate monthly price for each contact band. Essentials starts at $13, or £9.89 on the GBP view, for up to 500 contacts, and the same plan is $75, or £57.03, for up to 5,000. Standard starts at $20, or £15.21, for up to 500, and is $100, or £76.04, for up to 5,000. Premium’s smallest tier is 10,000 contacts, at $350, or £266.14. In the USD file, which Mailchimp calls the exact base, Startup at $17 is under every Mailchimp paid tier except Essentials at the 500-contact band, where Essentials is $13.

The third difference is what the login is for. Systeme.io’s FAQ puts the funnel, the course and a 0% platform transaction fee in the same account. Mailchimp’s help article describes audiences, templates, automations and support. It does not describe hosting a course or taking the course payment. A coach who needs the checkout is reading a different bill from a shop that only needs the letter. The wider set of email tools is on our Mailchimp alternatives page. The prices there were checked on 20 October 2026. This page is the head-to-head, rechecked on 14 November 2026.

Where Mailchimp is the better buy

When the work is the email, the list is still small, and you want the tools the free Systeme.io plan does not list.

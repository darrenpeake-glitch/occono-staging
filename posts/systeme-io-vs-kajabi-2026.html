Systeme.io vs Kajabi for Solopreneurs in 2026

Sam Calder Editor, Occono

Published 16 October 2026 · Last updated 20 October 2026 · Tools

Both platforms promise to handle funnels, email, and digital products in one place. The difference is mostly about cost, polish, and how much complexity you actually need.

How this comparison was made

This is desk research from vendor pages and public documents, plus the public reviews this article links. It is not a hands-on test. Nothing here was built for this page.

Our pick

Begin with Systeme.io

Who it suits: someone validating an offer who wants to start cheap, or free, and get to a working page without a premium monthly bill.

Pros

  • A usable free plan, a lower learning curve, and enough to test lead capture, email and a simple product.
  • The table puts the starting price at free, against about $149 a month for Kajabi.

Cons

  • A less polished student experience than Kajabi for courses and memberships.
  • Move only once the extra polish is clearly driving more sales. The article’s line is that starting expensive is harder to undo.
  • Systeme.io — better if you want to start cheap (or free), keep things simple, and get to a working offer quickly.
  • Kajabi — better if you already have revenue, want a more polished student experience, and are happy paying a premium.

Most solopreneurs should begin with Systeme.io. Move to Kajabi only once the extra polish is clearly driving more sales.

When Systeme.io wins

  • You’re validating an offer and don’t want monthly software costs yet
  • You need lead capture, email sequences, and a simple product delivery page
  • You prefer one tool that covers the core revenue path without extra complexity

When Kajabi wins

  • Your product experience (especially courses or memberships) is a key part of the value
  • You already have consistent sales and want better design and student tools
  • You’re willing to pay more for reduced friction on the delivery side

Practical recommendation

Launch and prove the offer on Systeme.io. Track conversion and delivery friction for 30–60 days. Only switch (or add) Kajabi if the current setup is clearly limiting sales or customer experience.

You can always migrate later. Starting expensive is harder to undo.

Neither platform is the right first choice if you only want to publish a newsletter. beehiiv is a newsletter platform for that narrower job, without the course and funnel tooling these two are built around.

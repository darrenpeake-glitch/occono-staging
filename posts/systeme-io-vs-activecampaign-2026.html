The short version

Systeme.io is an all-in-one for funnels, email, a course and checkout. ActiveCampaign is a marketing-automation and email CRM. The first bill is the difference. Systeme.io’s free plan is $0, and its top published plan in the visible FAQ is $97 a month. ActiveCampaign has no free plan. The trial is 14 days, with no credit card required, and the help article bases that trial on the Professional plan. After that, the cards say they are billed yearly. At 1,000 contacts, Starter’s USD yearly total in the catalog is $180, shown on the card as $15 a month.

The second difference is what the subscription contains. Systeme.io’s visible FAQ says unlimited email sends on every plan, including free, with the free plan capped at 1 email campaign and 1 automation rule. ActiveCampaign meters sends as a multiple of contacts: 10× on Starter and Plus, 12× on Pro and 15× on Enterprise. At 1,000 contacts the cards show 10,000, 10,000, 12,000 and 15,000 sends a month. The third difference is the sale. Systeme.io’s pricing page says 0% transaction fees and does not print Stripe’s or PayPal’s rates. The ActiveCampaign pages used here do not list a hosted course or a platform transaction fee. A coach who sells a workbook on ActiveCampaign still needs a checkout somewhere else, and this article does not price that second product.

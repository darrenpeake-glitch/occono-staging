Make vs Zapier for Solopreneurs in 2026

Affiliate note: Some links are affiliate links; we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Both tools move data between apps. The difference is how much power you get and how quickly the cost climbs.

Quick verdict

  • Make — better if you want more powerful scenarios and better value once you move past simple one-step automations.
  • Zapier — better if you prioritise the largest app library and the simplest possible interface.

For most solopreneurs who will eventually build multi-step workflows, Make is usually the stronger long-term choice.

Practical recommendation

Start with whichever tool already connects to the apps you use most. If both work, lean toward Make for better control and long-term cost.

Most one-person operations do not need both.

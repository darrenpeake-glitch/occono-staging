Make or Zapier: which saves a UK sole trader more time and money?

Affiliate note: Some links are affiliate links; we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Quick verdict

Best for a multi-step job on a small budget: Make. The free plan includes routers and filters, and the first paid plan starts at $9 a month for 10,000 credits.

Best for a two-step job you want live this afternoon: Zapier. The free plan is one trigger and one action, the app list is larger, and the builder is the simpler of the two. We do not earn from Zapier.

Which one fits you?

The complaints that show up in public reviews are the same ones a one-person business hits: the bill climbs, and the builder takes an evening you do not have. Milly B., quoted above, is a founder with one employee. Charles A. switched because the connection he needed was cheaper on Make, and he still said it takes a bit of learning.

A tradesperson chasing quotes. You sent the price, then you were on another job. One reminder from a spreadsheet is a two-step job: new row due, send one email. That fits Zapier’s free plan, inside 100 tasks a month. The quote chase on this site sends two different emails from one sheet, which is more than one action. Zapier’s free plan stops there. Make can branch that inside one scenario, until the two-scenario cap or the 1,000 credits run out. A search of the sheet spends credits before the email goes out.

A coach who has just sold a place. The payment should add a row, tag the person, and send the joining note. That is more than one action, so it needs Zapier Professional, from $19.99 a month billed annually for 750 tasks. On Make it is one scenario. Whether it stays on the free plan depends on how many module actions each sale actually runs. If the welcome note is the only step you care about, Zapier’s habit of not charging for the trigger, or for a filter, can keep the task count down.

A shop owner with one awkward app. If the tool that holds your orders is among Zapier’s 9,000+ apps and missing from Make’s 3,500+, Zapier is the one that saves the evening. If that app is marked premium, it still needs a paid Zapier plan. If both tools connect and the job is a single action, Zapier’s free plan is the faster setup. If you will add a second action next month, price Make’s Core plan at $9 before you accept Zapier’s $19.99.

Where Zapier is the better buy

Zapier wins when the job really is two steps, when you want it running the same day, and when the app you need is on Zapier’s larger list. It also wins on some logic-heavy workflows, because Filter, Formatter, and Path do not count as tasks, while a similar branch on Make spends credits. Trustpilot’s 1.3 from 323 reviews is the reason to watch the renewal. Turn off pay-per-task billing if you do not want an overage, and read the plan before the 14-day trial ends.

Make wins when the workflow has several steps and the volume is still modest. Two free scenarios and 1,000 credits cover a quote chase. Core at $9 for 10,000 credits is the first paid step if those two scenarios are not enough. The Trustpilot review from 19 September 2026 is the fair warning: the canvas is easier to get lost in, and a public review that month called the setup a waste of hours even with the built-in AI helper.

Pick one. Running both means two bills and two places for a broken login to hide.

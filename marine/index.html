OWNER QUESTIONS

Useful before you book.

What information should I send with a service enquiry?

Vessel type, engine make and model, serial number where available, boat location, the work required and any relevant history or symptoms.

Can servicing be completed at my marina or mooring?

Many routine and diagnostic jobs can be attended on location. Access, power, working space and the nature of the repair determine whether workshop facilities are more suitable.

Do you fit customer-supplied parts?

This is assessed case by case. Correct specification, quality, warranty responsibility and the risk of duplicated labour all need to be considered.

Can storage include servicing and launch preparation?

Yes. Storage can be combined with inspection, service work, cleaning, battery care and agreed preparation before launch.

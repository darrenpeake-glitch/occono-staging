Greyline Projects

Attract better-fit project enquiries and demonstrate delivery capability.

SectorConstruction & renovation
AudienceHomeowners and commercial clients planning substantial work
Visual directionConcrete grey · white · restrained rust
greyline-projects.example
The commercial problem

Design starts with what the website must fix.

Construction websites often present interchangeable service lists without helping clients judge project fit, process or quality.

Key decisions
  1. Lead with completed work and project scale.
  2. Separate renovation, extension and new-build routes.
  3. Explain the delivery process before the enquiry.
  4. Use restrained typography and architectural imagery.
Intended outcome

A structured, project-led website designed to generate more considered enquiries rather than a high volume of poorly matched leads.

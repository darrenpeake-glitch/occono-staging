Concept website case study

South Coast Marine

Turn broad marine enquiries into clear service, storage, repair and parts routes.

SectorMarine engineering & boat services
AudienceBoat owners across South Devon
Visual directionMarine teal · warm stone · service yellow
south-coast-marine.example
The commercial problem

Design starts with what the website must fix.

Marine businesses often provide many services but force every visitor through the same generic contact route. That creates uncertainty for the customer and incomplete information for the workshop.

Key decisions
  1. Lead with the customer outcome rather than a list of capabilities.
  2. Separate service, storage, repairs and parts into distinct journeys.
  3. Ask for vessel, engine, location, symptoms and preferred timing.
  4. Use real coastal and workshop imagery to establish local credibility.
Intended outcome

A calm, credible website that makes the breadth of the business easier to understand and produces a more useful first enquiry.

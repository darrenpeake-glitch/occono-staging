Concept website case study

The Salt House

Create desire, establish place and encourage direct booking.

SectorHoliday accommodation
AudienceCouples and small groups planning a coastal stay
Visual directionSea blue · chalk · weathered stone
the-salt-house.example
The commercial problem

Design starts with what the website must fix.

Accommodation sites must sell an emotional experience while still answering practical questions about the property, availability and location.

Key decisions
  1. Let photography carry the first impression.
  2. Balance atmosphere with clear property facts.
  3. Keep availability and direct-booking actions visible.
  4. Use a quieter editorial layout suited to the pace of the brand.
Intended outcome

An atmospheric direct-booking website that makes the stay feel distinctive while keeping the practical booking route easy to find.

Concept website case study

Oddments

Create a memorable shop while keeping product discovery and purchase simple.

SectorSpecialist ecommerce
AudienceDesign-conscious customers buying small independent products
Visual directionRust · lilac · warm cream
oddments.example
The commercial problem

Design starts with what the website must fix.

Small ecommerce brands need personality, but visual playfulness cannot obscure product information, price or the route to basket.

Key decisions
  1. Use a strong editorial product grid.
  2. Give maker stories a clear supporting role.
  3. Keep price and basket actions immediately legible.
  4. Build a visual language distinct from the service-business sites.
Intended outcome

A playful but usable shop that proves Occono can adapt its visual system to a very different voice and buying journey.

AIMi Backup

AIMi Backup is a private utility operated by Darren Peake. It saves encrypted backups of his own home server's essential configuration and application data to his own Google Drive account.

Why Google Drive access is needed

The utility uses Rclone to connect to Google Drive and Restic to encrypt backups before upload. Google authorisation allows it to create backup files, read them back, check their integrity and restore them when needed.

It requests the drive.file permission, limiting access to files created by, or explicitly made available to, this app. It also checks the authorised account identity and available storage. It does not request access to Gmail or Google Photos.

Personal use

This is for the operator's own account. It is not a public backup service, does not offer customer sign-ups and does not give website visitors access to the home server or its backups.

Privacy and contact

Read the AIMi Backup privacy notice for information about access, storage, retention and revoking authorisation.

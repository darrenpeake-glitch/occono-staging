Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Who we are

Occono is an independent web studio based in Devon and Cornwall and run by Darren Peake. The final business postal address and registration details will be added before commercial launch.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, business name, email address, project requirements and any other information you choose to provide. Basic technical server logs may also record information such as browser type, approximate location and IP address.

Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals.
  • To deliver and administer agreed services.
  • To maintain security and diagnose website problems.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Lawful basis

We generally process enquiry information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, or because we have a legitimate interest in responding to potential clients. Contract and legal-obligation grounds may apply after work begins.

Sharing and processors

Information may be handled by carefully selected providers used for email, hosting, forms, analytics, accounting or project delivery. The final provider list will be confirmed before launch. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Unsuccessful enquiry information will normally be removed when it is no longer reasonably required. Client and financial records may be retained for longer where needed for contracts, support, taxation or legal claims.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to request transfer of your information. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent to hello@occono.co.uk once the business inbox is active.

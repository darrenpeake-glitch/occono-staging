Useful answers

Before we talk.

Are the portfolio websites real?

They are demonstration builds created to show complete design, content and development capability across different sectors. They are labelled honestly rather than presented as invented client work.

Do I need to provide all the words?

No. We use your knowledge of the business, then help structure and write the core website content. Specialist or regulated claims remain subject to your approval.

Can you replace an existing website?

Yes. Existing content can be audited, retained where useful and rebuilt into a clearer structure with a stronger visual system and customer journey.

Will I own the website?

Yes. The agreed finished website, its content and your domain remain under your control. Any platform or third-party limitations are made clear before the build.
